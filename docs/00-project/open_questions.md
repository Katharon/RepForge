# Open Questions

## Blocking before production branding

1. Trademark availability for `RepForge` in target markets.
2. App Store / Google Play name availability/reservation for `RepForge`.
3. Final app subtitle and store keywords.

## Not blocking MVP implementation

1. Exact Premium price and trial length.
2. Whether Premium is monthly only or monthly + yearly.
3. Exact first official catalog size.
4. Whether custom exercise muscle metadata is optional or required before it appears in muscle-balance analytics.
5. Whether age is entered as date of birth, birth year, or direct age value.
6. Minimum target age for the app and wording for minors.
7. Exact safety/disclaimer wording for onboarding and recommendations.

## Current answers

- MVP = tracker + groups + analytics.
- No paid cloud database for official exercise catalog.
- Official exercises shipped as versioned assets and patches.
- Equipment filtering and max-load constraints must be modeled.
- App starts with system locale and falls back to English.
- UI is inspired by Setgraph, not copied.
- Monetization direction = freemium with Premium coach features.


## Resolved in v6

- Product name: RepForge.
- English and German must ship from MVP start.
- Ads are not part of MVP.
- JSON catalog assets are canonical and imported into Drift on first launch/update.
- Baseline avoids paid cloud/hosted services and recurring developer-controlled infrastructure costs.
