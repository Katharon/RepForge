# Naming Candidates

## Status

These are preliminary product-name candidates for the app. They were intentionally chosen as coined or uncommon compound names. A quick exact web search against Google Play and Apple App Store result domains did not return obvious matches at generation time, but this is **not** a legal or store-reservation guarantee.

Before choosing a final app name, run:

1. Exact search in App Store and Google Play by target country.
2. App Store Connect / Google Play Console name reservation checks.
3. Trademark search for EU/Austria and any future target markets.
4. Domain and social-handle checks.
5. Pronunciation check in German and English.

## Stronger candidates for this project

The names that currently fit best are:

- **MyoNexa** — technical, muscle-related, modern.
- **RepNava** — short, app-like, less generic.
- **SetLuma** — softer, visual, analytics-friendly.
- **MyoAxis** — strong fit for balance, muscle load, and progression.
- **RepFoundry** — powerful, but more developer/tool-like.
- **VolumeForge** — strong for advanced lifters, but more niche.

## Naming guidance

Avoid names that are too generic, such as `Gym Tracker`, `Workout Log`, or `Strength App`. They are hard to rank, likely already taken, and weak as a brand. A coined name plus a descriptive subtitle is better, for example:

```text
MyoNexa — Workout Tracker & Training Analytics
```

Store metadata can explain the product while the actual app name stays distinctive.
