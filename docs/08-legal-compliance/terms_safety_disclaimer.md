# Terms and Safety Disclaimer Baseline

## Tone rule

RepForge must use hedging for guidance:

Use:

- may
- could
- appears to
- suggests
- estimated
- might be useful
- consider
- based on your logged data

Avoid:

- you must
- you are injured
- you are not recovered
- you are overtrained
- you are forbidden to train
- this will guarantee results

## Safety disclaimer baseline

RepForge provides workout tracking, analytics, and educational training suggestions. The app does not provide medical diagnosis, medical treatment, or individualized medical advice. Training recommendations are estimates based on logged data and may be incomplete or inaccurate.

Users should stop or reduce training if they experience pain, dizziness, injury symptoms, or unusual discomfort. Users with medical conditions, injuries, pregnancy, or uncertainty about training safety should consult qualified health or fitness professionals.

## Coach wording examples

Bad:

```text
You are not recovered. Do not train chest today.
```

Good:

```text
Your recent logs suggest your chest may still be under recovery load. A lighter push session or a different focus could be useful today.
```

Bad:

```text
You need another set.
```

Good:

```text
If this set still felt controlled, an additional back-off set could help maintain volume today.
```

## Subscription wording baseline

Premium features must be described clearly:

- what is free
- what is Premium
- price and billing period
- trial length if any
- renewal behavior
- cancellation path
- restore purchases
- terms and privacy links

## No medical-device positioning

RepForge must not market itself as a diagnostic, therapeutic, rehabilitation, or medical decision-making product unless a future legal/regulatory review explicitly approves that direction.
