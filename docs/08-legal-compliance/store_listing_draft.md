# Store Listing Draft

This is a working App Store / Google Play metadata draft for RepForge. It is
not legal advice and must be reviewed against the final app behavior, privacy
policy, screenshots, store availability, and trademark status before public
release.

## Shared product facts

- App name: `RepForge`.
- Category: health and fitness / workout tracker.
- MVP position: local-first workout tracking, workout groups, exercise logging,
  base progress analytics, local backup/export.
- Data stance: no account required, no ads, no analytics SDK, no cloud sync, no
  developer collection of workout history in the MVP baseline.
- Training wording: use `progress`, `trends`, `estimated`, `signals`, and
  `readiness`; avoid medical, diagnosis, injury-prevention, or guaranteed-result
  claims.
- Premium stance: current local tracking, groups, base analytics, custom
  exercises, and export remain free. Future guidance/recommendation features may
  be Premium only after their explicit slices.

## English draft

### Subtitle / Short Description

Workout Tracker & Progress

### Promotional Text

Log workouts quickly, organize training days, and understand your progress with
local-first analytics.

### Full Description

RepForge is a local-first workout tracker built for lifters who want fast set
logging and clear progress signals without needing an account.

Create workout groups such as Push, Pull, Legs, Upper, Lower, Full Body, or your
own custom split. Log weight, reps, labels, and notes quickly, then review
progress by exercise, session volume, estimated strength trends, and weekly
training load.

RepForge is designed around privacy and ownership. Your workout history stays on
your device unless you choose to export or import a backup. The MVP baseline
does not require an account, cloud sync, ads, or analytics tracking.

Training insights are estimates and trends, not medical advice. RepForge helps
you review your training and make better next decisions, but it does not
diagnose conditions, prevent injuries, or guarantee results.

### Keywords

workout tracker, strength training, gym log, weight lifting, fitness log,
progress tracker, training journal, sets reps, workout groups

### Privacy Summary

RepForge is local-first. In the MVP baseline, workout and profile data stay on
the device. The app does not require an account, does not include ads, does not
include an analytics SDK, and does not sync training data to a developer
backend.
