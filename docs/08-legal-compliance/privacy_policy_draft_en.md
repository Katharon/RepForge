# Privacy Policy Draft EN — Local-first MVP

> Note: This is an engineering draft, not legal advice. Review before publication.

## RepForge

RepForge is a local-first workout tracking app. By default, workout data is stored on the user's device.

## Data stored in the app

Depending on usage, the app may store locally:

- exercises and workout groups
- workout sets with weight, repetitions, timestamps, and notes
- settings such as language, units, and theme
- optional profile data such as age, sex, body weight, and training goal
- equipment inventory, including available equipment and maximum usable load

In the MVP, this data is not transferred to the developer.

## No account and no cloud in MVP

The MVP does not require an account. It does not include cloud sync, a cloud database, advertising, remote analytics, or remote crash reporting.

## Local storage

Data is stored locally on the device. If the app is deleted, local data may be lost unless it has been exported or backed up first.

## Export and import

Export/import features are explicit user actions. The user decides where
exported files are stored or shared, and exported files may contain sensitive
training and profile data.

## Premium and payments

If Premium features are enabled, purchases are handled through the relevant app store. RepForge does not directly process payment details.

## Health and training guidance

RepForge provides training analytics and suggestions. These are estimates and do not replace medical advice. If you experience pain, injury, or medical concerns, consult qualified professionals.
