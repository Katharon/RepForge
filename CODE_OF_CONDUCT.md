# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

RepForge should be a focused, respectful project space for building a local-first workout tracker.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Keep feedback specific, actionable, and tied to the project goals.
- Assume good intent while still correcting mistakes clearly.
- Respect privacy and do not share personal training, health-adjacent, or account data without consent.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Spam, off-topic promotion, or disruptive conduct.
- Pressure to add services or tracking that conflicts with the documented local-first MVP decisions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and contributions that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in restricted participation.
